Privacy Policy for Revati

Effective: July 24, 2026 · Last updated: October 22, 2026 (AI consent and answer reports)

Revati ("we", "our", "us") is published by Msunjay Enterprises. This policy explains what the Revati : Kundli & Panchang app collects, where it goes, and how you can delete it. It covers the app on Android and on iPhone, which are the same app and handle your data the same way.

This app was previously published as "AstroVeda". It is the same app under a new name.

1. The Short Version

  • An account is optional. Everything works without one. If you sign in (Google, Apple or email), your name, email address and your saved Kundali profiles are stored with Google Firebase so you can back them up and restore them (section 3a). You can delete the account and its backup from the app.
  • Festival and birthday emails. A signed-in account gets an email on the morning of a big festival, and on the birthday of anyone in its backed-up profiles (section 3a). Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Panchang, Rashifal, Kundali, Guna Milan, Muhurat and numerology are calculated on your phone. Your birth details are never sent anywhere to produce them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob (section 4), and counts which screens and features are used and reports crashes through Google Firebase (section 3b) — never what you typed.
  • Revati PRO is an optional yearly subscription bought through Google Play or the App Store, one free day of it for watching a video ad (section 3c), or a period of it for referring friends (section 3a). The optional AI features send only what they need to answer (section 3c).
  • Nothing you enter — no name, birth detail or saved profile — ever leaves your phone unless you share it yourself, type a place name to look up, sign in and back it up, ask an optional AI question or send an AI report (section 3c). The ads never see any of it.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade any information, and we have none to sell.

2. Information the App Keeps on Your Phone

  • Birth details. The name, date, time and place of birth you enter for a Kundali, a Guna Milan match or a saved profile, and your recent searches. They are kept in a database on your phone, encrypted with a key held by the phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore, iPhone Keychain). The key is never included in a backup, so a copy of the database is unreadable anywhere else.
  • Settings. Your language, rashi, city and reminder choices.
  • A free day of PRO. When it started and when it ends, if you earned one by watching a video ad (section 3c). Nothing else is recorded with it.
  • Reminders. Panchang, Muhurat and festival reminders, and up to four muhurats you ask to be reminded of, are scheduled on the phone itself, not sent from anywhere.
  • Your questions about a chart. If you ask the AI about a birth chart, the last 20 questions and answers for that chart are kept in the same encrypted database as your birth details, so a follow-up can build on them. "Clear conversation" under that chart, or deleting all data, removes them. They are not included in a backup.

None of this is sent to us unless you sign in and back up (section 3a), or ask a follow-up chart AI question, which can send its last two questions and answers to Google as described in section 3c.

3. What Leaves Your Phone

  • Place look-ups. When you type a place of birth, or tap "Current location" and allow it, the app asks your phone's own geocoding service to turn the name or the coordinates into a town. On Android that service is provided by Google, on iPhone by Apple; their policies are at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ The app reads your location only while it is open and only when you ask, and keeps only the town and its coordinates, on the phone.
  • App updates. When it opens, the app asks Expo's update service (expo.dev) whether a fix to its own code is available. That request carries the app's version, the platform and a random identifier Expo makes for this installation, and none of your data.
  • Ads. Requests to Google AdMob, described in section 4.
  • Your account, if you make one — section 3a.
  • Usage counts, crash reports and announcements — section 3b.
  • PRO and the AI features, if you use them — section 3c.
  • Sharing is yours to start. A share card, a chart picture, a Guna Milan PDF or a profile file leaves the phone only when you share it through your phone's share sheet, to the app you pick. The app never sends a message itself and never reads your contacts. - A share card shows only what its screen shows: a day's Panchang and city, a rashi's reading, a muhurat, or a Guna Milan score. Names appear on a Guna Milan card only if you turn on "Include names" for that card; birth dates, times and places never appear. - Each card carries a link and a QR code to our download page. The link says only which kind of card it came from (for example `?s=rashifal`), never anything about you or the person you send it to. Whoever opens it visits our website, which uses no cookies and no analytics; the page then sends them to Google Play with that same card kind attached, so Play can count installs that came from shared cards.

3a. Sign-in and Cloud Backup (optional)

Signing in is never required. If you choose to:

  • Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. It receives your email address and name — from Google, from Apple ("Sign in with Apple", which lets you hide your real email) or as you type them — and, for email sign-in, your password, which Firebase stores only as a hash we cannot read.
  • Cloud backup stores your saved Kundali profiles (name, date, time and place of birth, coordinates and notes) in Google Cloud Firestore, under your account only. The database rules let no other user read or change them, and no person at Revati reads them. The one exception is an automated job that reads each backed-up profile's name and date of birth, and nothing else, to send the birthday email below.
  • An account list. Your email, name, when the account was made and when you last synced are kept so that the app's administrators can find an account, answer support email and stop abuse. An account that abuses the service can be barred from backing up.
  • Backups happen only when you sign in or tap "Back up and sync now". Nothing is uploaded in the background.
  • Emails. On the morning of twelve big festivals (Makar Sankranti, Maha Shivratri, Holi, Ram Navami, Hanuman Jayanti, Raksha Bandhan, Janmashtami, Ganesh Chaturthi, Navratri, Dussehra, Karwa Chauth and Diwali), and on the birthday of anyone in your backed-up profiles, an automated job sends one email to your account's address. It goes through Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which receives your email address and the email's text, including that person's name. There is never more than one email a day, and none on other days. Each email has an unsubscribe link, and after you unsubscribe you get none. Brevo's policy is at https://www.brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy/
  • Google's policy is at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Refer friends (optional). If you open "Refer friends" while signed in, the app makes you a referral code and keeps, in the same database: your code; for each friend who used it, their account's id, whether they came through your link or typed the code, and when; and each reward you reached, its status and, if it was declined, the reason. It also keeps when a new account first signed in, so that only an account under 7 days old can use a friend's code. On Android, when the app was installed from a shared Google Play link, it reads the link's referral code from Google Play and fills it in for you; it counts only when you tap to use it. You see only how many friends joined, never who they are. The app's administrators see the friends' email addresses behind a reward so they can check it before approving it, and a reward is given only when an administrator approves it. On Android a granted period of PRO is kept against your account. On iPhone an approved reward is an App Store offer code, shown to you in the app and redeemed in the App Store, under Apple's terms; the app keeps the code with the reward and Apple handles the redemption. A reward is never given for a rating or a review.

3b. Usage Statistics, Crash Reports and Announcements

  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): which screens are opened and which features are used (for example "a Kundali was made"), with the app version, device model, operating system, language, country (estimated by Google from the IP address) and a random identifier Firebase makes for this installation. Also counted: when a full-screen ad could have appeared, whether it did and, if not, why (for example "PRO is on"); how a rewarded video ended; the value Google estimates each ad earned; and each AI request's outcome and size in tokens, which is what it costs — never its words; and when a share card is previewed or handed to the share sheet, which kind of card it was, its language and its shape, and whether the share sheet reported a choice — never what was on the card, and never who it went to. Never a name, birth detail, city, profile or anything you type. The advertising ID is not collected for this, and the data is not used for ad personalisation.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): when the app crashes or a screen fails to open, the technical details of the failure, the device model, operating system, app version and the same installation identifier.
  • Announcements (Firebase Cloud Messaging): so the app can receive the occasional message to all users, Firebase gives this installation a notification token. It stays with Firebase; we never receive it or keep a list. Turn notifications off for Revati in the phone's settings to stop them.
  • Firebase keeps analytics data for 2 months and crash reports for 90 days. Google's policy is at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3c. Revati PRO and the AI Features (optional)

  • Purchases. PRO is sold by Google Play and the App Store; they handle the payment and we never see your card or bank details. RevenueCat, which checks that a subscription is genuine, receives the store's purchase record and a random identifier for this installation — not your name or email. RevenueCat's policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • A free day for a video. Watching a rewarded video ad to the end turns PRO on for 24 hours, at most once a day. The day is kept only on your phone (section 2); no account is needed and nothing about it is sent to us. The video itself is an ad like any other (section 4).
  • AI answers (Firebase AI Logic, Google's Gemini models). Before the first Numerology question, birth-chart question or personalised horoscope insight, Revati shows a consent sheet naming Google Gemini, reached through Firebase AI Logic, and the information that feature sends. Tapping Allow stores the current disclosure version on this phone separately for that feature. Tapping Cancel, dismissing the sheet or leaving it without allowing sends no AI request and does not use the AI allowance. If the permission record cannot be saved, the request is not sent. Only after permission, and only when you tap for an answer: - a Numerology question sends your question, app language and today's date. When Numerology has been calculated, it also sends the name, date of birth, Moolank and its ruling planet, Bhagyank, name number, friendly numbers and unfriendly numbers shown on that screen; - a birth-chart question sends your question, app language and today's date, the chart's lagna, Moon nakshatra, each planet's name, sign, house, degree and retrograde status, current mahadasha and antardasha planets and their start and end dates, and up to the last two questions and answers of that chart's conversation. Not the name, the date, time or place of birth — but these positions are worked out from them, so someone with an ephemeris could work the date and approximate time of birth back out; - a personalised horoscope insight sends the selected rashi, requested daily, weekly or monthly period, that period's reading, app language and today's date. It does not add a name or birth details; - Time and place of birth and your saved profiles themselves are never sent. Anything personal you type into a question is sent as part of that question; - the news card sends only the request for the news, with no personal data. Google processes these prompt data to produce the answer; Revati does not store them outside your phone. Only a report you choose to send, including an optional selected-answer excerpt, is stored in `ai_reports` as described below.
  • Reporting an AI answer (optional). You can open the options beside a generated answer, choose Report, select a reason, optionally enter details and tap Send report without leaving Revati. An existing signed-in account is required to send a report. Opening or cancelling the report form sends nothing. The optional selected-answer excerpt is off by default, shown in a preview and included only if you turn it on. Reports go to the `ai_reports` collection in Google Cloud Firestore and contain your account ID, answer type, app language, chosen reason, optional detail, optional selected-answer excerpt and a server timestamp. Revati does not automatically attach your question, saved birth profile, chart context or full conversation. Any personal information you choose to enter in the detail or include in the excerpt is part of that report.
  • Up to five reports from one account are accepted per UTC day. App clients, including app administrators, cannot read, change or delete reports. An authorized Firebase Console/IAM operator reviews reports and manually deletes them within 30 days; there is no automatic report-cleanup job. Deleting your account does not automatically delete a report already sent. You can ask supportrevati@gmail.com to request its manual deletion.
  • App Check. So that only this app can ask for AI answers, the phone proves it is a genuine install through Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest (iPhone). No personal information is part of that check.

4. Advertising

Revati is free and is paid for by ads, served by Google AdMob. To choose and measure an ad, Google's software in the app sends Google information about the device — the advertising identifier where you allow it, plus the IP address, device model, operating system and app version — and how the ad was seen or tapped. Google's policy is at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

  • On iPhone the app asks Apple's "Allow tracking?" question once. Only if you allow it are your ads personalised and the advertising identifier used; if you do not, the ads stay non-personalised and every feature stays the same. You can change the answer at any time in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in the phone's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • In the EEA, the UK and other places where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and "Ad privacy choices" in Settings lets you change your answer.
  • Kinds of ads. A banner between cards; now and then, after you finish a Kundali, a match or a numerology reading, a full-screen ad, which may be a video; and a rewarded video that plays only when you tap "Watch a video" on the PRO screen. Google tells the app only whether that video was watched to the end, so the free day can be given. There is no ad when the app opens, and none while PRO is on.
  • Ads are rated for teens and above, and the app does not direct them at children.
  • Your birth details, saved profiles and searches are never given to AdMob or anyone else.

5. Permissions

  • Location (while the app is in use) — only to set the city for Panchang timings, and only when you tap "Current location".
  • Notifications — for the reminders you turn on, and announcements.
  • Internet — for place look-ups, app updates, ads, usage statistics and crash reports, announcements, and sign-in and backup if you use them.
  • Advertising ID (Android) — used by Google AdMob, as described in section 4.

6. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete any saved profile inside the app at any time.
  • Settings → Delete all saved data removes every saved profile and recent search the app has stored on your phone. It cannot be undone. It does not delete a report you already sent; see section 3c.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone.
  • Settings → Cloud backup → Delete account & data deletes your account, its cloud backup and its entry in the account list, at once and for good. Profiles saved on the phone stay. Reports you already sent are not deleted automatically; see section 3c. You can also email us to have it done.
  • If an administrator ever blocked an account from backing up, the record of that block (the account's id, its email address, the reason and the date) is kept after the account is deleted, so the decision can be checked later. It is never shared and is used for nothing else.
  • Referral records (a code, who used it, rewards and their decisions, a granted period of PRO, and when the account first signed in) are kept after an account is deleted, by account id only, so the same reward cannot be claimed twice. They hold no birth details and are used for nothing else.
  • Signing out stops syncing and leaves the backup in place until you delete the account. For what Google holds about ad requests, see https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy

7. Children

Revati is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and its ads are not directed at children.

8. Changes

If Revati ever adds a feature that sends your information anywhere else, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
